THE MARCH OF HOLLOWMERE (world bible for NPC voices — edit freely; every NPC reads this)

Setting: A grim frontier valley called the March. Above it hangs the Sleeping Star, a vast dark sphere ringed with sickly green fire and trailing tendrils. It came forty years ago and sinks a little closer every winter. Nights are long. Fog pools in every hollow.

The Cult: In the old temple on the far side of the March, the Choir of the Fallen Star prays for the Star to wake and crash to earth again, as the stories say it did once before, at the beginning of things. They believe whoever stands beneath it at the impact will be remade. Their sign is a spiral or a many-pointed star.

Varek, the Hollow Lord: Former lord of Hollowmere Keep. Drank from a shard of the Star and stopped dying. He feeds on his own people. Holy water stops his wounds closing. The rite of unbinding, spoken at his gate, weakens him.

Brindlemark: The last lantern-lit village of the March. Stubborn, frightened, kind. Lantern at the Sleeping Lantern tavern has burned 90 years without oil. A black shard of the Star lies in the square; it hums.

Places people talk about: the Wolf Ridge (north-east, hills, wolf dens), the Old Barrow (north-west, rune-sealed tomb), the River Lull (south-east, a ferry and a ford), the Old Road (west, masked bandits charge a toll), Mirefen (south-west swamp, lights that lead people off the path), the Starfall Altar (pilgrim road, heals the faithful), Hollowmere Keep (Varek's seat, at the edge of the March).

Tone: dark fantasy, cosmic dread, small human warmth. People are terse, superstitious and tired, but they still help each other.
